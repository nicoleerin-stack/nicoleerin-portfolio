  • Case Study: Digital Legacy Clinic SOP Design

    Standardizing client support workflows for student teams.

Abstract: This case study documents the creation of a comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for the CU Digital Legacy Clinic, an end-of-life service that helps clients organize and preserve their digital assets. The project addresses a key operational challenge: inconsistent workflows across student teams that led to gaps in documentation, unclear responsibilities, and inefficiencies in client support. Through process mapping, stakeholder feedback, and iterative refinement, the final SOP establishes a clear, step-by-step structure for client intake, digital inventory, archival work, quality checks, and final deliverables. The result is a reliable, repeatable workflow that strengthens service continuity and improves the clinic's ability to support clients effectively.
