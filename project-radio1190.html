  • The Informatics of Radio1190

    An information flow analysis of communication, organizational structure, and process improvement at a student-run radio station.

Information Ecosystems  |  November 2026

RoleResearcher / Information Systems Analyst
TimelineNovember 2026
SkillsInterview Research, Social Worlds Mapping, Data Journeys, Miro, Process Analysis, Organizational Communication

Abstract: This project examined the information flow and communication processes at Radio 1190, a student-run volunteer radio station at CU Boulder. As the station navigates post-pandemic changes, we analyzed how information moves through different levels of the organization and identified areas for process improvement.

We conducted interviews with key station members, including the general manager, chief engineer, student volunteer manager, and co-music directors. Using our social worlds model, we mapped the station's structure at micro, meso, and macro levels, incorporating data sources, stakeholder influences, and secondary resources such as the DJ Canvas course, studio observations, Discord server, and station website.

A data journey was created in Miro to document key processes, including DJ training, on-air procedures, communication channels, and station operations. We also explored challenges related to automation, underwriting, and fundraising, as well as opportunities to improve internal workflows and volunteer engagement.

This project provided insights into how information is shared within the station and helped inform recommendations for optimizing communication, training, and organizational processes.
