Abstract: This project identifies an opportunity for a tavern-style pizza parlor with a full bar in an underserved area of New Orleans. Using SQL for data analysis, we examined Yelp, map, and restaurant data to pinpoint ideal locations, optimize hours, and analyze consumer trends. Sentiment analysis of reviews informed service strategies, while seasonal and foot traffic trends guided launch planning. Findings provide a data-driven foundation for a successful and sustainable business venture.
