  • Our Blue Planet's Refuges

    An interactive data story exploring Marine Protected Areas, biodiversity, and reef health across Hawaii.

View scrollytelling website

Read full Medium writeup

Abstract: This project analyzes the ecological impacts of Marine Protected Areas (MPAs) in Hawaii by combining Reef Check biodiversity observations with NOAA's MPA Inventory and global protected-area datasets. Examined historical growth in MPAs, compared protected and nearby non-protected reefs, and visualized species abundance and reef-quality metrics through interactive charts and maps. These insights were presented in a custom scrollytelling website designed to guide viewers through temporal, spatial, and ecological patterns.
