  • Student Interactions with Buff Portal

    A user research and survey analysis project examining student usage patterns, engagement drivers, and experience gaps.

Abstract: This study examines student usage patterns of Buff Portal, focusing on the frequency and purpose of access. Specifically, it explores whether the reasons for using Buff Portal influence usage frequency and how the ease of finding resources impacts engagement. Data collection involved a structured survey designed collaboratively, distributed through various CU Boulder student networks, and administered via Qualtrics. Responses were analyzed using univariate and bivariate methods to identify key trends and correlations. Findings provide insights into student interactions with Buff Portal, highlighting potential areas for improving accessibility and user experience.
